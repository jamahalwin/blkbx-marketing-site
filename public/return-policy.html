CUSTOMER CARE

Return & Reservation Refund Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

At BLKBX LLC, we want every customer to feel confident in their purchase. This policy explains how refundable reservations, order cancellations, product returns, and refunds are handled.

1. Pre-Production Reservations

Certain BLKBX products, including THE NIGHT, may be offered for reservation before manufacturing begins.

A reservation requires a $10 payment, which is credited toward the final purchase price if the customer proceeds with their order.

A reservation does not guarantee that a product will enter production, become available for purchase, or be delivered by a particular date.

BLKBX will communicate relevant updates regarding product availability and next steps.

2. Reservation Cancellations and Refunds

Customers may cancel a pre-production reservation and request a full refund of their $10 reservation payment at any time before completing the final product purchase.

To request a refund, email support@blkbx.co and include your reservation reference number.

If BLKBX decides not to proceed with production or cannot fulfill the reserved product, BLKBX will refund the reservation payment in full.

Reservation refunds will be issued to the original payment method. We aim to initiate approved refunds within seven business days of receiving the request or confirming cancellation. Additional time may be required for the payment provider to credit the customer's account.

No physical product needs to be returned to receive a reservation refund.

3. Physical Product Returns

Once BLKBX products become available for purchase and delivery, customers may request a return of an eligible product within 30 calendar days of confirmed delivery.

To qualify for a change-of-mind return, the product must be unused, in its original condition, and returned with its original packaging and included accessories.

Customers must obtain return authorization before shipping merchandise back to BLKBX.

4. Nonreturnable Items

The following items are generally ineligible for change-of-mind returns:

  • Personalized or custom-engraved products.
  • Opened or consumed food products, including chocolates.
  • Products damaged through customer use or mishandling after delivery.

These exclusions do not eliminate applicable rights or remedies for defective, damaged-on-arrival, or incorrectly supplied products.

5. Return Authorization

To initiate a physical product return, contact support@blkbx.co with:

  • Your order or reservation reference number.
  • The product being returned.
  • The reason for the return.
  • Photographs, if the product arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect.

If the return is eligible, BLKBX will provide return authorization and shipping instructions.

Do not send merchandise to a BLKBX business or mailing address without receiving return instructions.

6. Return Shipping

For approved change-of-mind returns, customers are responsible for return shipping costs.

If a product arrives defective, damaged, or incorrect, BLKBX will provide an appropriate remedy and cover necessary return shipping costs where applicable.

We recommend using a trackable shipping service for customer-arranged returns.

7. Physical Product Refunds

Once an authorized return is received and inspected, BLKBX will determine whether it qualifies for a refund under this policy.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method. We aim to process refunds within seven business days after receiving and inspecting the returned merchandise.

Payment providers may require additional time to post the refund.

Customers will receive an email when their return has been processed.

8. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact support@blkbx.co as soon as reasonably possible.

Please include your order reference and photographs of the issue, where available.

BLKBX will review the situation and arrange an appropriate remedy, which may include a refund or replacement, subject to availability and applicable law.

9. Policy Updates

BLKBX may update this policy as its products and operations evolve.

The policy applicable to an order or reservation will be determined by the terms provided at the time of the transaction, subject to applicable law. Updates will not retroactively reduce rights already granted to customers.
